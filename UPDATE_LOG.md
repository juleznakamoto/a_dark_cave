# Update log

Notes for players. Newest first. Copy a dated section when you publish an update.

## Unreleased

### Added

- After you lose an attack wave, you can spend 1000 Insight once to push its return back 10 minutes, and again only after you lose that wave once more.

### Changed

- Hovering a build button highlights that building in the side panel when you already have one. An upgrade highlights the building it replaces until the new one is built.
- Hovering a building on the map highlights its name in the side panel with a fading underline and a dot in the middle. The line stops around the dot and around a letter it would cross. The number after a building name stays plain.
- Madness visions tend to arrive sooner once you qualify for them.
- Purchase tooltips show the cost first, then what you gain. The achievement badge says it unlocks the title and the description.
- Each Longhouse and each Fur Tent now adds 0.25% to the chance a new villager arrives.
- Preparing for Frostfall now costs fur as well as wood and food. The fur price rises each time you face it.
- Enter CRUELWORLD in Settings to unlock Cruel Mode. It can be activated when you start a new game. This is not recommended for new players.
- Once you have 10 Stone Huts, special finds while exploring the cave and while hunting show up a little more often. Veinroot stays the same.

### Fixed

- The ground around the village on the map is clear, so a blood moon shows through it.
- Hovering Feed Fire highlights Wood in the side panel, and hovering Call Merchant highlights Gold.
