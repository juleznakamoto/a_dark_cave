# Update log

Notes for players. Newest first.

Steam and iOS each have a log. Most notes are shared: write those once under Shared. A note only one version would show goes under Steam or iOS. Phone layout that the Steam version does not have goes under iOS. When you publish, the Steam notes are Shared plus Steam, and the iOS notes are Shared plus iOS. Copy that pair for the date you are shipping.

## Unreleased

### Shared

#### Changed

- When a village job is full, its /max turns yellow like a full resource.
- The Wandering Collector only appears after you have seen gold.
- Village − and + job buttons and insight marks are a little larger.
- After you stay paused for 15 seconds, the red resume button pulses with a soft glow.
- On the village map, the Black Estate outline is the same thickness as other buildings.
- The building sound plays when you start construction, not when the building finishes.
- After you finish Sleep, random events wait 30 seconds before they can start again.
- Mining sounds are a little quieter.
- Mining sounds play a little slower for each later ore under Mine, from Stone through Moonstone.
- Feed Fire sounds play a little slower at higher Heartfire levels.
- Dialogs from the top-right menu fade the game behind them to solid black.
- Settings and Stay Connected use the same padding inside as other dialogs.
- When you point at a resource in the side list, or press it on a phone, its amount turns bold.
- While paused, the time beside Pause tells you it is the play time of this playthrough.
- The language you have selected is shown in red.
- On the village list, the Disgraced Prior row shows how many actions he is assigned to, labeled Disgraced Prior Actions.
- The line under Tireless Worker names the Disgraced Prior, so you can see the skill is his.

#### Fixed

- Achievement descriptions are no longer cut short; progress like 3/5 now sits after the text instead of in the middle of the row.
- Loading a save while you are already asleep no longer replays the sleep sound or fades the mist in from empty.
- During sleep and the blood moon, the top and bottom bars stay solid instead of letting the mist or smoke show through.
- Sleep mist fades in smoothly instead of jumping brighter in steps.
- Item tip descriptions stay a readable width: they still hug longer effect lines, but short ones no longer squeeze the text into a tall column.
- After you press and hold an action button on a phone to read its tip, tapping that button again runs the action instead of doing nothing.
- Tips stay a little in from the edge of the screen instead of sitting flush against it.
- The Coinhouse tip no longer lists +2% Lucky Chance; that is the starting rate, and only Bank and Treasury list the bonus.
- A new timed event tab flashes green again, and the tab and timer flash red when little time is left.
- On pact choice cards, a long quote no longer sits against the line above the Madness cost.
- On a phone, pact choice and cruel mode marks in the header show their tip while you press and hold, like other tooltips.
- On the start screen, a sentence that wraps onto a second line still dissolves when you light the fire.

### iOS

#### Added

- Claiming an achievement reward or improving an Estate skill gives a medium haptic tap.
- Collecting a silver or gold coin on the village map, or turning event log filters on or off, gives a light haptic tap.
- The iOS app gives short haptic feedback on key moments such as lighting the fire, trading, events, combat, and sleep. You can turn it off in Settings.

#### Changed

- On a phone, building outlines on the village map are a little thicker.
- On a phone, language, ambience, and sound on the start screen are a bit larger.
- On a phone, the header sits closer to the left and right edges of the screen.
- On a phone, dialogs have a bit less space inside between the frame and the text.
- On a phone, dialogs sit farther in from the left and right edges of the screen.
- On a phone, language, ambience, and sound on the start screen sit in the upper left, at the same height as the header icons.
- On a phone, the language list on the start screen is larger.
- When you share from the iOS app, the message says you are currently playing A Dark Cave, followed by the App Store link.
- The iOS start screen no longer shows The Hustle logo, or the social, FAQ, About, Press, and legal links.
- The start screen clouds fill the whole iPhone screen, including around the clock and the home indicator.
- In the iOS app, Save stores the progress card in your photos, and Copy puts that image on the clipboard so you can paste it. Share opens the share sheet.
- On a phone, a building you tap on the map stays marked until you leave the Map tab.
- On a phone, and in the iOS app, the bottom bar is gone. Pause is at the top left, and your play time shows beside it while the game is paused. Feedback is in the menu. On a phone, Stay Connected sits under Feedback.
- In the iOS app, the progress card has no store line. Share includes the App Store link. The invite link is not there.
- The iOS app has no text size setting. Text stays at the normal size.

#### Fixed

- Leaving the app and coming back no longer leaves the ambience music silent until you restart.
- Leaving the app and coming back no longer freezes the game when Sleep is unlocked.
- The area above the header matches the header background, and the header stays clear of the status bar.
- On the village map, tapping a building in the side list clears the building you had selected on the map, so only one stays highlighted.
- The language icon on the start screen opens on the first tap instead of needing a second one.
- Insight badges animate as soft round shapes again, instead of spinning triangles.
- Locking the phone no longer makes a short beep when the music stops.
- On a phone, tapping a villager job or the play time beside Pause shows what it means.
- A newly unlocked tab pulses brighter and dimmer again, instead of staying solid white.
- On a phone, tapping a construction queue slot or a villager preset slot shows what it does.
- The first time you open the app, it uses your iPhone's language.
- Opening Control Center, switching apps, or getting a notification no longer shows a music player for the game.
- In Settings, the music and sound sliders no longer show a red dot at the right end of the track.
- On a phone, the resources an action needs light up only while that action's tip is open.
- On a phone, tapping the pact choice rings in the header shows what they mean. The Cruel Mode mark does the same.

## 2026-11-16

### Shared

#### Added

- When your madness is high and you carry more than 100 silver, five children may stare at you from between the trees. Walk on, or approach them.
- The header shows five red rings for pact choices. Each choice you have unlocked fills one ring, from left to right.

#### Changed

- Estate upgrade bars and the attack-wave bar rest a little softer, and turn solid while you hover them. The smoke in the bar under your pointer moves eleven times as fast.
- The Odd Elixir can appear once you have 4 Wooden Huts and the Crude Bow. Building more huts no longer skips it.
- While Staring Deer is active, hunting also brings in twice the fur and bones, the same as food.
- The Madness tooltip now says that ill events follow those dangerous paths.
- Achievement descriptions for a full set no longer say how many books, companions, tools, or pacts you need.
- Later achievements pay 100 gold, and some mid-game ones pay 750 Insight. The earliest ones still pay silver.
- Prime Foundry and Master Tannery add 1, and Masterwork Foundry and High Tannery add 2, in every version of the game.
- Hovering a resource shows its capacity first, with the number on the right of that line, in yellow when the stack is full.
- In Cruel Mode, trying to free the slave trader's captives can now fail.
- Updated Sleep dialog UI.
- The cycle ring on the village panel fills more smoothly.

#### Fixed

- The Great Fire countdown pauses while a dialog is open.
- Calling the merchant no longer replaces an event that appears while he is on the way. He arrives after you finish that event.
- Closing a cube memory or another event no longer brings the same dialog, or the next one, straight back.
- Starting a new game keeps your music and sound volumes.
- Bastion building tooltips and warehouse capacity lines use your language.
- The message you share with a progress image is in your language.
- Claiming an achievement no longer shifts the rows below it.
- Effects of some events/items in code where not matching displayed effects:
    - Deepening the First Flame gives you 8 housing and a 15% crafting discount.
    - Raven's Orb gives 5 Knowledge.
    - Selling two villagers to the slave trader raises your Madness.
    - Letting the Blackened Mirror's time run out counts as refusing the offer.
    - Choking Fog shows a 25% production cut.
- While you sleep, steel, leather, and insight add up across cycles, only whole units go into your stores, and a gain shows a decimal only while it is still under 1.

### Steam

#### Added

- Text size settings include Huge.

#### Changed

- On Linux, the window stays a normal size. Pick Huge when the writing looks small.
- Fix Linux scaling issues when using Proton.

#### Fixed

- Pact choice cards keep each line of writing in its own space at Huge text size, and the cards grow a little wider as the text gets larger.
- The Improve button, Claim button, shop filters, village job buttons, and build-queue marks grow with text size.

## 2026-11-06

### Shared

#### Added

- You can play the game in Turkish. Thank you to Kuroiteiken.
- After you lose an attack wave, you can spend 1000 Insight once to push its return back 10 minutes, and again only after you lose that wave once more.

#### Changed

- Pact choice cards have a little less space between the border and the text.
- Hovering a build button highlights that building in the side panel when you already have one. An upgrade highlights the building it replaces until the new one is built.
- Hovering a building on the map highlights its name in the side panel with a fading underline and a dot in the middle. The line stops around the dot and around a letter it would cross. The number after a building name stays plain.
- Madness visions tend to arrive sooner once you qualify for them.
- Purchase tooltips show the cost first, then what you gain. The achievement badge says it unlocks the title and the description.
- Each Longhouse and each Fur Tent now adds 0.25% to the chance a new villager arrives.
- Preparing for Frostfall now costs fur as well as wood and food. The fur price rises each time you face it.
- Enter CRUELWORLD in Settings to unlock Cruel Mode. It can be activated when you start a new game. This is not recommended for new players.
- Once you have 10 Stone Huts, special finds while exploring the cave and while hunting show up a little more often. Veinroot stays the same.

#### Fixed

- The Obsidian Orb button, the huntress and crow bonus lines, and relic and blueprint names in costs and rewards now use your language.
- Fur tents show how many you have built.
- On the pact choice screen, you can scroll to the title and Continue when the window is short or zoomed in. The scrollbar stays hidden.
- The fading line under a damaged fortification's name disappears when you move the pointer away.
- The ground around the village on the map is clear, so a blood moon shows through it.
- Hovering Feed Fire highlights Wood in the side panel, and hovering Call Merchant highlights Gold.
