A Dark Cave – Text-Based Survival and Incremental Settlement Game

A Dark Cave is a text-based survival and settlement-building incremental you can play for free in your browser on desktop or mobile. Light a fire in an ancient cave, gather resources, recruit companions, and uncover what lives below. Inspired by minimalist incrementals such as A Dark Room, Kittens Game, and Candy Box. Also on Steam with a free demo.

An Incremental Survival RPG

Inspired by A Dark Room, Kittens Game, and Candy Box. Settlement building, resource management, turn-based combat, and a dark fantasy atmosphere. Semi-idle plus active: you make choices and manage a settlement. Not a clicker and not a second-screen idle.

Gather, Craft, Upgrade

Collect resources, craft tools and weapons, upgrade buildings and skills through multiple tiers. Assign villagers to jobs, unlock new schematics, and expand your settlement to survive what lies ahead. A Dark Cave features a deep progression system with wood, food, stone, and rare materials gathered from the cave and surrounding areas. Craft tools like axes and picks, then weapons and armor for turn-based combat. Build structures including the workshop, barracks, and shrine, each unlocking new capabilities. Villagers can be assigned to gathering, crafting, or construction tasks. Schematics unlock advanced items like the arbalest, nightshade bow, and stormglass halberd. The settlement grows from a single fire to a fortified village as you push deeper into the world. Your settlement can keep producing resources while you are away, and the game saves progress automatically in your browser for both desktop and mobile play.

Fellowship Companions

Recruit companions with their own storylines. A wandering knight, an aging wizard, a wildling huntress bound by blood oath, and many more. Each is earned through choices and changes how you play.

Explore a Dark World

Venture into ancient forests, forgotten ruins, and hostile lands. Encounter strangers, face attack waves, and confront things that should have stayed buried.

Combat & Crafting

Fight enemies in turn-based combat with crafted weapons, bombs, and combat skills. Build defenses to protect your settlement from what comes out of the dark.

Dark Fantasy Atmosphere

The story unfolds through events, choices, and discovery. Build shrines and monuments. Unlock forbidden knowledge. Some choices strengthen your people. Others lead to madness.

Play for Free in Your Browser

Play for free in your browser on desktop or mobile. Progress saves locally. Also on Steam with a free demo.
